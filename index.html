Meet your podiatrist

About Linda

BSc.Pod.Med · F.Inst.Chp · HCPC Registered Chiropodist & Podiatrist

Linda Pearson is an HCPC-registered chiropodist and podiatrist, in practice since 1997 — over 25 years of experience caring for feet across North Wales. A Fellow of the Institute of Chiropodists and Podiatrists, she also served ten years as its president.

The practice welcomes new patients as well as existing ones, and offers care for the whole family — including children's foot care and specialist diabetic foot care. The premises are accessible, with plenty of parking. A range of quality foot care and eco-friendly products is also available at the clinic.
