Your privacy

Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 2026

This notice explains how Linda Pearson Podiatry collects and uses personal information through this website. It is written to be clear and honest about exactly what happens to any details you share.

Who we are

Linda Pearson Podiatry is a private podiatry practice based at Unit 7, Abakhan Craft Village, Coast Road, Llanerch Y Mor, Holywell CH8 9DX. For any question about your data, contact us on 07907 106006 or lindapearsonchp@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

If you use the enquiry form on this site, we collect the name, phone number and message you provide. We use these solely to respond to your enquiry and, if you go on to book, to arrange your appointment. We do not use them for marketing, and we do not sell or share them with anyone for their own purposes.

Please avoid including detailed medical information in the enquiry form ‐ a brief description of how we can help is enough, and any clinical details are best discussed by phone or in person.

How the form works

Enquiry submissions are processed and stored by Netlify, the service that hosts this website, which notifies us by email when a new enquiry arrives. Netlify processes this data on our behalf; you can read how they handle it in their own privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

Other services this site uses

To display properly, this site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on the contact page, an embedded Google Map. Both involve your browser connecting to Google, which means Google may receive your IP address and set cookies. This is standard for websites using these services; you can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

When you first visit, we ask whether you're happy to accept these cookies. If you decline, the map is not loaded and you can still find us using the address above. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page.

If you accept cookies, we also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use this site - for example which pages are read most. This tells us nothing that identifies you personally, and we do not use it for advertising. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting treatment relationship, after which they are deleted. Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment are removed once they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted. To make any of these requests, contact us using the details above and we will respond promptly.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.
